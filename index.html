Lingzhi Yang

I'm a PhD candidate in Computational Applied Mathematics at Stony Brook University. I work on distributed systems for long-horizon scientific agents, local agents, and anti-forgetting.

My research combines agentic reinforcement learning with systems for building, executing, and verifying scientific workflows. I also develop inference software and programming tools for dataflow AI accelerators, including Cerebras CS-3.

I'm affiliated with Stony Brook IACS, the Stony Brook Bioinformatics Lab, and Brookhaven National Laboratory.
